### Friends & Your Privacy ###

Friends lets people you trust stay connected with your Pill Buddy in a fun, privacy-friendly way.

Friends can:
- See what your Pill Buddy is wearing
- See your Pill Buddy's facial expression<sup>1</sup>
- Send your Pill Buddy friendly pokes, hugs and funny interactions

Friends cannot:
- See what meds you're taking or how often you're taking them
- See your health stats, what you're tracking, when you're tracking them and what their values are

<p class="footnote"><sup>1</sup> Friends can see when your Pill Buddy is upset, which happens whenever you miss something. This allows them to send you encouragement. To protect your privacy, they cannot see the list of your meds or exactly what you are missing.</p>
